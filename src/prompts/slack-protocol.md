Use `slack://` for read-only Slack context: the root lists workspaces; `<workspace>/channels/` lists your channels; `<workspace>/channels/<name-or-id>?limit=&oldest=&latest=` reads history; `<workspace>/threads/<channel>/<ts>` reads a thread; `<workspace>/users/<id-or-me>`, `/canvases/<id>`, and `/files/<id>` read records; `/search?q=...` searches public content, with `scope=all` for public and private or `type=files` for files. Send or mutate Slack content only through `xd://slack`.
